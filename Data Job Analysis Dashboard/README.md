## Introduction
I wanted to explore the data job market to understand how salaries vary across different factors, which positions and skills are trending, and which platforms are most effective for finding data-related jobs.  
To answer these questions, I used a dataset from DataNerd.tech and built an interactive Excel dashboard analyzing the 2023 data job market.  

### Questions Explored  
1. What is the salary range for data jobs?
2. Which job platforms offer the most opportunities?
3. How strong is the demand for data-related roles?
4. What are the most in-demand skills?
5. How common are remote job opportunities?

### Excel Skills Used
- Power Query > Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Power Pivot > Data Modeling and Table Relationships
- Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts > Data Analysis and Visualization
- Excel Formulas and DAX
- Dynamic Dashboard Design


## Dashboard Overview
The dashboard allows users to dynamically filter insights using:
- Job Title
- Country
- Job Type  

All KPIs and visualizations update automatically based on the selected inputs.

### KPIs Included
1. Median Salary
2. Top Hiring Platform
3. Total Job Count
4. Top Skill
5. Remote Job Count

## Key Insights
- Compensation levels vary widely across data roles, with senior and specialized positions showing significantly higher earning potential compared to entry-level roles.
- A small number of job platforms dominate job postings, indicating that candidates can improve their job search efficiency by focusing on a few key sources.
- Job demand is not evenly distributed across regions and roles, with some countries offering more opportunities than others and certain roles appearing more frequently, reflecting where hiring activity is most concentrated in the data job market.  
- The most in-demand skills are associated with varying salary levels, and the top skills are not necessarily the highest paid, showing how skill demand and compensation are distributed across the market.
- Remote opportunities exist but are limited in proportion, suggesting that traditional work arrangements still dominate the data job market in 2023.

## Conclusion
This project provided a structured view of the 2023 data job market by transforming raw job posting data into an interactive Excel dashboard. It highlights how salary levels, job demand, and skill requirements vary within the industry and helps identify practical insights for job seekers, such as where opportunities are concentrated and which skills are most valuable.

Overall, this analysis demonstrates how data visualization and modeling in Excel can turn complex datasets into clear, decision-oriented insights that support career planning in the data field.
